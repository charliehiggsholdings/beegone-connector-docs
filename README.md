# BeeGone — Claude Connector

Get instant bee-removal quotes and request visits from **BeeGone**, the UK's live honey bee
removal specialists (est. 2008, 10,000+ removals, fully insured), directly from your AI
assistant. Bees are relocated alive wherever possible — never exterminated.

**Connector URL:** `https://beegone-agent-mcp.charlie-4be.workers.dev/mcp`
**No sign-in required.** Works with Claude custom connectors (web, Desktop, Mobile, Claude Code).

## What it can do

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **BeeGone quote questions** | Loads the live quote calculator's questions for your situation — where the bees are (chimney, wall, roof, tree, attic…), property details, and the three service plans. Read-only. |
| **Instant bee removal guide price** | For chimney, wall and roof colonies: an instant guide price for all three plans — **Pollen** (no guarantee), **Nectar** (4-year guarantee), **Honey** (10-year guarantee) — VAT and travel included. Read-only; creates no records. |
| **Request a BeeGone visit or callback** | Files your request with BeeGone. A specialist always phones to confirm details and the final figure before any work — **no payment is ever taken through the assistant**. Locations that can't be priced online become surveyor callbacks, and you can also book a consultation yourself at [beegone.co.uk/book-a-consultation](https://beegone.co.uk/book-a-consultation/). |

## How a conversation goes

1. Tell your assistant where the bees are and it asks the same questions BeeGone's own
   calculator asks (how long they've been there, property age, access, and so on).
2. For quotable locations you get the three plan prices instantly. Estimates are confirmed
   by a BeeGone specialist on the phone before anything is booked.
3. If you'd like to go ahead, confirm you're happy to be contacted and agree to BeeGone's
   [Hive Rules](https://beegone.co.uk/privacy-policy/) — your assistant then files the
   request and BeeGone calls you back. Prefer a human at any point? Call **0800 852 7336**.

## Important notes

- **Bumblebees are protected.** They are not removed; the connector provides advice for
  living alongside a temporary nest instead.
- Nothing is charged online and no card details are ever requested through the assistant.
- The connector never signs you up to marketing.
- Data handling: your answers, postcode and (only when you request a visit) your contact
  details go to BeeGone's own booking systems to prepare your quote and callback — the same
  systems the website calculator at [book.beegone.co.uk](https://book.beegone.co.uk/) uses.
  See the [privacy policy](https://beegone.co.uk/privacy-policy/).
